## Impact vs Urgency vs Priority

**Impact** - How many people or how much of the business is affected.

**Urgency** - How fast it needs fixing, i.e., how time-sensitive it is.

**Priority** - The order you work things in. It's calculated from "**impact x urgency**", not picked.

It's important to know:
- Priority is calculated, not picked from a personal choice
- The loudest caller isn't automatically high priority; a VIP with a minor issue is still low impact.

----------

## SLAs

SLA (Service Level Agreement) - A promised target between the service provider and the customer:
- **Pauses** - "On Hold" states such as "Awaiting Caller", "Awaiting Vendor" or "Awaiting Change".
- **Breach** - The timer hit 100% before resolution

-------

## L1 vs L2 vs L3

**L0** - Self-Service (Portal, KB, password reset tool)
**L1** - Service desk (Logs the ticket, does first-line fixes, e.g., Password Resets, Basic Troubleshooting) and escalates if stuck
**L2** - Specialists or desktop support. Deeper troubleshooting, such as software issues, device problems and Active Directory
**L3** - Engineers, developers or vendors. Root cause, code fixes, architecture, server and network work.

----

## Incident vs Request vs Problem vs Change

**Incident** - Broken, restore service
**Request** - The user wants something, via the catalogue
**Problem** - Root cause behind repeat incidents
**Change** - Planned alteration to the work environment (Standard, Normal, Emergency)

----

## FCR

**FCR (First Contact Resolution)** - Tickets resolved at first contact / Total tickets.

To prevent a failure of FCR:
- **Confirm with the user before resolving and document the fix properly**

----

## Processes and ITIL

**ITIL** - The framework of best practices for IT service management. Most service desks loosely follow it.

**ITSM** - Running IT as a service. **ServiceNow** is an ITSM tool itself.

**Triage** - Assessing a ticket, then categorizing, prioritizing and routing it.

**Escalation**:
- **Functional** - To a more skilled team
- **Hierarchial** - To management, e.g., for an unhappy customer/major incident

**RCA** - Root cause analysis; finding out why it happened, not just fixing the symptom

**Post-incident review** - What happened, what worked and what we changed

**CAB (Change Advisory Board)** - The group that reviews and approves normal changes.
