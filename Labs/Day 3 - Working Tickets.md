## Cancelling Tickets

It's important to always CANCEL or CLOSE tickets, instead of deleting them. Deleting tickets will remove the audit trail, and thus our opportunity to prove that this specific issue has happened before. 

Note that a ticket will be permanently closed and added as a knowledge article, to view at any time upon closing:
