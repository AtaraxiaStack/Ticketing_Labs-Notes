The Lifecycle. I should remember this:

**New → Assigned → In Progress → Pending → Resolved → Closed**

1) **New** - Ticket just came in. No-one's working on it just yet.
2) **Assigned** - An owner or group has received the ticket. They will likely look at it shortly.
3) **In Progress** - Someone is actively working on it right now.
4) **Pending** - The progress is blocked, and input from a user/third party is wanted. (The SLA pauses)
5) **Resolved** - You believe it's fixed. You give a resolution code, as well as notes saying what you did.
6) **Closed** - No more changes. It's finished!
7) **Cancelled** - Also can occur. Usually happens when a ticket is raised by mistake, a duplicate or the issue fixed itself.

This lifecycle is NOT a straight line. You can go from **"In Progress"** to **"Pending"** several times.
