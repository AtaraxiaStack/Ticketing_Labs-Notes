**ITSM (IT Service Management)** - This refers to a set of services delivered to the business. These services include:
- Planning
- Delivering
- Supporting 
- Improving 

ITSM focuses primarily on managing the entire lifecycle of IT services, ranging from email and network access to software rollouts and hardware requests.

---------

The dominant "framework" ITSM adopts is known as **ITIL (Information Technology Infrastructure Library** (We are currently on ITIL 4!)

-------

# ITIL

**ITIL (Information Technology Infrastructure Library** - A framework that helps organizations align their digital/IT services with business goals.

The part I need to know the most is the **Service Value Chain** ITIL 4 adopts.

Here it is below:

``Plan → Improve → Engage → Design & Transition → Obtain/Build → Deliver & Support``

**"Deliver & Support"** is the service I need to know the most for ticketing.
